## How the problems work

Each problem is marked with a level: **Beginner**, **Intermediate**, or **Advanced**. The problems
get harder in that order. Every practice page in this course uses the same three levels.

To answer a problem, select **Start this in the editor**. The editor on the right opens with a short
template. Write your answer on the lines that start with `//`. In TypeScript, a line that starts
with `//` is a **comment**: text for people to read, which the computer ignores.

When you finish a problem, select **Reveal solution** to compare your answer with a model answer.
Your answer does not need to match it word for word.
